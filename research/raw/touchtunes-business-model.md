# touchtunes-business-model

QUERY: TouchTunes business model revenue share bar owners operators licensing music jukebox industry

## Synthesized answer
TouchTunes installs its digital jukeboxes in bars, restaurants and nightclubs at no upfront cost to the venue, then collects a per‑song fee that includes the public‑performance licensing payments it has negotiated with record labels, music publishers and PROs such as ASCAP, BMI and SESAC; after deducting those licensing costs and any equipment‑service charges, the remaining revenue is split between the venue owner and the local amusement‑machine operator, typically with the venue receiving roughly a quarter to one‑half of the net proceeds (often described as a fixed 24 % of gross revenue after a small monthly reimbursement) and the operator retaining the balance, while TouchTunes retains the song‑fee revenue and benefits from the licensing agreements.
