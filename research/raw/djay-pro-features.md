## Synthesized answer
djay Pro AI includes Algoriddim’s Neural Mix™ technology that separates tracks into four real‑time stems—vocals, harmonics, drums and bass—allowing full control of each source on any deck; an AI‑enhanced Automix (Automix AI) that automatically detects intros, outros and rhythmic patterns to create seamless, production‑style transitions; support for up to four decks with a dedicated four‑deck layout and crossfader‑fusion that can blend any combination of the isolated stems; a full suite of hardware integration features including MIDI mapping, pre‑cueing, DVS (AI‑driven digital vinyl control), and compatibility with major controllers and mixers; advanced audio tools such as a sampler, slicer, up to three chainable audio effects per deck, XY‑Pad, instant FX, and Sugar Bytes effects; a high‑precision Fluid Beatgrid for beat‑matching, beat‑grid editing, and pitch‑stretching; video mixing capabilities with visual effects, photos and titles; plus additional DJ utilities like looping, cue points, slip mode, waveform zoom, and a built‑in sequencer/looper for creating live beats and remixes.
