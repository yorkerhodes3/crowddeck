# touchtunes-technology

QUERY: TouchTunes jukebox hardware Playdium Virtuo Angelina architecture cloud connected

## Synthesized answer
TouchTunes’ current hardware line—Playdium, Virtuo (and Virtuo II) and Angelina—are all built as modular, service‑friendly digital jukeboxes that rely on a cloud‑based platform for music catalogs, real‑time analytics, remote updates and venue‑operator management; Playdium’s design includes interchangeable field‑replaceable units such as backplane, payment‑door and camera modules and is wired for broadband or wireless Ethernet via CAT‑5 or Wi‑Fi, Virtuo and Virtuo II feature a snap‑in part architecture with a removable motherboard, HD 24‑ or 27‑inch touchscreens, adjustable LED lighting and optional photobooth or karaoke add‑ons, while Angelina takes the modular concept further with a removable motherboard and integrated lighting and visualizer systems, all three models connecting to TouchTunes’ cloud services to stream licensed music, push software and lighting updates, and deliver usage data to operators.
