# rekordbox-features

QUERY: Pioneer rekordbox DJ performance mode features CloudDirectPlay Lighting mode plans

## Synthesized answer
Pioneer’s rekordbox DJ performance mode includes two key subscription‑based features: CloudDirectPlay and Lighting mode. CloudDirectPlay, launched with firmware 2.0 for the CDJ‑3000, lets a DJ log into their rekordbox account on the player and stream their entire cloud‑based library directly from the hardware without needing a USB drive, preserving all metadata such as Hot Cues and Memory Cues and automatically syncing any edits back to the cloud; this capability is available to users of the rekordbox Creative and Professional subscription plans, which start at $14.99 per month and also provide Cloud Library Sync. Lighting mode, which automatically generates lighting cues based on track phrases and allows full DMX‑512 control via the optional RB‑DMX1 interface, is bundled with the Professional plan, enabling DJs to design, save and trigger synchronized light shows directly from rekordbox.

# CloudDirectPlay Announced For Pioneer DJ CDJ-3000s. Users of Pioneer DJ’s CDJ-3000 media players can now play their music directly from the cloud, in a new feature announced today called CloudDirectPlay. DJs who subscribe to either the Creative or Pro Rekordbox software plan can use the feature by exporting music to the cloud using their software when preparing their sets. That music can then be accessed from CDJ-3000 players as if it were on a locally inserted USB drive. To enable the feature, users can update their CDJ-3000s to firmware v2.0 from the usual download page on the Pioneer DJ website. This isn’t access to streaming services like Beatport or Beatsource, and it isn’t music over WiFi.
