# touchtunes-app-features

QUERY: TouchTunes app features Fast Pass Play Next bar finder song queue loyalty rewards

## Synthesized answer
The TouchTunes app lets users locate nearby bars, restaurants and other venues on an interactive map, browse the full jukebox queue in real time and add songs to play, while offering a “Fast Pass” option that lets you spend credits to jump ahead and hear your track sooner; it also provides a song‑upgrade feature, displays each song’s position in line, and includes a loyalty program (TouchTunes Rewards) that awards points and free credits for each paid play, lets you earn perks and redeem them for future songs.
