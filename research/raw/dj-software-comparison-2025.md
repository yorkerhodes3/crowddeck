## Synthesized answer
In the 2025 landscape the leading DJ‑software choices can be differentiated by platform coverage, pricing model, hardware integration, and flagship features: Serato DJ Pro (Windows & macOS) retails for roughly $179 perpetual (or $129 / yr) and is prized for industry‑standard DVS, exceptionally responsive scratching, extensive plug‑and‑play support for virtually any USB controller, and recent addition of key‑shift and AI‑driven track‑analysis, though it lacks a native video engine; Rekordbox 7 (Windows & macOS) costs about $129 perpetual and offers the tightest integration with Pioneer CDJ/XDJ gear, a powerful preparation workflow, built‑in stems separation, cloud‑sync for playlists, but its video capabilities are limited and its performance UI is less customizable; Traktor Pro 4 (Windows & macOS) is priced around $119 perpetual, shines with modular controller mapping, a rich suite of FX and remix decks, robust beat‑grid editing, and strong community presets, yet it provides no native video mixing and relies on third‑party tools for CDJ connectivity; djay Pro AI (Windows, macOS, iOS, iPadOS) follows a subscription model (~$19 / month) and distinguishes itself with AI‑driven stem separation, seamless Spotify/Apple Music integration, cloud‑based library sync, and an intuitive touch‑optimized UI, though its effects library is narrower than the pro‑tier competitors; VirtualDJ 2025 (Windows & macOS) offers a free Lite tier and a full‑feature Pro license (~$299) that includes comprehensive video mixing, VST plug‑in support, karaoke, and one‑click mapping for thousands of controllers, making it the most versatile but also the most feature‑dense and price‑heavy option; finally, Mixxx (Windows, macOS, Linux) is 100 % free, open‑source, and supports virtually any MIDI controller with fully editable mappings, provides basic stem analysis, and includes essential DJ tools such as sync and cue, though its UI is less polished, it lacks official cloud or AI features, and updates depend on community contributions.
