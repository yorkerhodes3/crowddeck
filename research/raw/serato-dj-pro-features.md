# serato-dj-pro-features

QUERY: Serato DJ Pro features list Stems Practice Mode DVS expansion packs 2025

## Synthesized answer
Serato DJ Pro’s 2025 feature set includes full Stems support that lets you isolate and remix vocals, instruments, bass and drums on‑the‑fly and apply Stems Pad FX such as Echoes and Brakers, a Practice Mode that lets you mix two or more tracks without any hardware connected, native DVS compatibility for turntables and CDJs (enabled through the optional DVS Expansion Pack), and a suite of expansion packs that add specialized functionality – the DVS Expansion Pack for turntable control, Serato Video for synchronized video playback and VJ‑style effects, Serato Play for advanced laptop‑only DJing, plus additional packs like the FX Pack, Remix Pack and other genre‑specific add‑ons; the core software also offers 4‑deck mixing, up to 50 built‑in FX, Sampler slots, Beat Jump, Slicer, Quantize, Smart Sync, Key Analysis, and MIDI mapping, giving professional DJs a comprehensive toolbox for creative performance.
