# digital-jukebox-competitors

QUERY: AMI Entertainment Rowe digital jukebox competitors NEXTGEN jukebox features comparison

## Synthesized answer
AMI Entertainment’s NEXTGEN line, exemplified by the NGX‑32 and NGX Curve models, distinguishes itself with interchangeable “Smart Skins” that physically wrap the unit and can trigger custom graphics and interactive features, a fully networked core that supports remote route‑management via the Co‑Pilot app, integrated music‑video playback of more than 35,000 videos, free‑to‑play trivia games, ad‑upload capabilities, and a swappable core design that simplifies maintenance; its competitors – Pandora CloudCover, NSM Entertainment Network, Rockbot, Edge226 and Soundtrack – focus primarily on cloud‑based music streaming and licensing platforms that deliver curated playlists, DJ‑style automation, digital signage or background‑music services, and in some cases revenue‑sharing ad models, but they generally lack the dedicated hardware‑centric features of NEXTGEN such as interchangeable skins, on‑site video jukebox capability, built‑in trivia, and the operator‑focused remote‑management suite that lets venues control volume, schedule content, run promotions and track detailed route reporting from a single mobile interface.
