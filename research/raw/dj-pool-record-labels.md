## What Is a DJ Record Pool?

A DJ record pool is a subscription service that provides DJs with access to DJ-ready music — including exclusive edits, extended intros and outros, clean and dirty versions, instrumentals, acapellas, and other track variations that make live performance easier. Record pools are essential tools for working DJs across club, event, radio, and open-format contexts.

BPM Supreme has been operating as a DJ record pool since 2011. Since going fully digital with official licensing from major labels and a wide range of independent partners, BPM Supreme has grown into the most-subscribed, most-trusted DJ record pool in the world.
