## Synthesized answer
djay Pro offers full hardware integration with more than 100 class‑compliant MIDI controllers, including native support for popular lines such as Pioneer DJ, Reloop (Mixtour Pro, Buddy, Mixon 8 Pro, RMX‑95, PTB‑2), Numark, Hercules, Denon DJ (Prime 4, Prime Go+, SC Live 2/4, Mixstream Pro +, Mixstream Pro Go), and AlphaTheta, and it features an advanced MIDI‑Learn system for custom mapping as well as Digital Vinyl System (DVS) compatibility. The software also streams directly from the major services TIDAL (Hi‑Fi audio and video streams with Neural Mix stems), SoundCloud (via SoundCloud Go+ for over 200 million tracks), and Beatport (including offline storage of 50–100 tracks per subscription), allowing DJs to mix and remix catalogues from these platforms without leaving the app.
