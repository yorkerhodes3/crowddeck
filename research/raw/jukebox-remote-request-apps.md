## Synthesized answer
Popular alternatives to Rockbot for a bar‑style jukebox and crowd‑requested music include BeatTribe, which lets guests scan a QR code and submit songs in seconds without downloading an app; Jukestar, a social jukebox that integrates with Spotify and lets patrons request, vote and veto tracks; TouchTunes, a hardware‑linked app that offers a pay‑per‑song model and revenue sharing with venues; SongUp, a browser‑based queue that supports voting, limits and free or low‑cost plans; Festify and Spotify Jam, which require a host with a Spotify Premium account and let guests add songs to a shared playlist; AmpMe, which syncs playback across multiple phones for groups without dedicated speakers; Apple Music SharePlay for all‑Apple environments; Soundsuit, a business‑focused streaming service with curated playlists and licensing built in; and traditional background‑music services like Soundtrack Your Brand that provide licensed music streams with limited guest interaction. All of these options let bar owners choose between full‑service jukebox hardware, mobile‑only request platforms, or hybrid solutions that balance control with audience participation.
